Have an idea for a gadget, gift, display, or one-off tool? EWB builds custom electronics to order, from a single blinking thing to a finished device in a custom enclosure.

## Good fits

- Custom displays, clocks, and scoreboards
- Macro pads and small input devices
- LED pieces for walls and shelves
- Restoring or modding vintage electronics

## How it works

1. **Send an inquiry.** Tell us what you have in mind. A rough idea is fine.
2. **Get a quote.** You'll get an estimate for price and timeline, usually within a few days.
3. **Deposit and build.** A deposit starts the build. You'll get progress updates along the way.
4. **Delivery.** Pay the balance and your device ships (or is picked up locally).

## What to include

It helps to know:

- What you want it to do
- Rough size, look, or where it will live
- Your budget range
- When you'd like it by
